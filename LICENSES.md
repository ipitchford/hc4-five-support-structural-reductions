# Licence map

- Original prose, structured claims, metadata and original data: CC0-1.0.
- Original source code: MIT License.
- Complete operative notices: `LICENSE.md`.
- Material under `source/`: its existing upstream terms; inclusion does not
  relicense it.
- Bibliographic material, cited papers, external software, names and
  trademarks retain their existing legal status.

When a file-specific notice conflicts with this map, the file-specific notice
controls.
