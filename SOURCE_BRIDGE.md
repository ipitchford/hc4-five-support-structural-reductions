## Boundary of the bridge

Reconstructing an algebraic system from upstream code does not establish the
mathematical conclusions. Conversely, the release's exact certificate checks
do not certify every upstream claim or every possible normal-form convention.
The bridge is deliberately narrow: pinned input, explicit transformation,
frozen encodings, and claim-specific replay.
