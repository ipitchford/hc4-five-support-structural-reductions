# Corrections

## 2026-10-27 — errata to hc4-five-support-structural-reductions 0.1.0-candidate (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22216400)

The archived PDF, the manuscript source, the Zenodo deposit and the tag `v0.1.0-candidate` are unchanged. These errata were confirmed by the Evidence Press archive audit and are also shown at <https://evidencepress.org/releases/hc4-five-support-structural-reductions/>.

1. Corrected the ring in Section 5. The archived text places the 17 generators of the unreduced ideal in a 14-variable ring. In the unreduced coordinates the ring has 18 variables: I = (F1, …, F17) ⊂ ℚ[f3, …, f10, g0, …, g9], including g4, g5, g7 and g8. Four of the equations eliminate those variables, leaving 13 equations in 14 homogeneous variables.

   The computation used the correct rings. No theorem, certificate, computation or stated result is affected.

2. Updated a bibliography entry. The archived reference to Weyman is listed as “ahead of print”. It is Weyman, Jerzy. 1993. “Gordan Ideals in the Theory of Binary Forms.” Journal of Algebra 161: 370–391. https://doi.org/10.1006/jabr.1993.1225.

   This is a citation correction only. No theorem, certificate, computation or stated result is affected.
